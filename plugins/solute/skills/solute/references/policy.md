# Solute policy

Sol owns intent, design, integration, hard debugging, verification, and judgment.

Use Luna xhigh only for bounded work that is quick to brief and cheaper to check than do. Fits: searches, mechanical edits, implementation to existing contracts/tests, test isolation, and first review. Sol keeps ambiguity, architecture, unlocalized failures, coupled integration, tiny tasks, and completion decisions.

Sol contracts, Luna executes, Sol verifies. Spawn `gpt-5.6-luna` at `xhigh` without full history. Brief one deliverable, relevant paths/context, constraints, and success check. End: `Return only outcome; changed paths or file:line evidence; verification and result; real blocker or risk. Do not narrate or paste available diffs.` Parallelize only independent work.

If placement is unclear, read `delegation-guide.md`. Stop stalled workers; do not auto-retry.

Write plainly and specifically. If the user says "Don't use /solute", ignore this policy for that turn.
